# Modeling Under Uncertainty

Coursework for Modeling Under Uncertainty at Cornell Tech, Fall 2026.

## Skills showcased

- **Monte Carlo simulation:** estimating the mean and variance of cycles to failure with 100, 1,000 and 10,000 samples, and seeing how the estimates converge.
- **Simulation-based optimization:** choosing how many hours a vendor should work to maximize expected profit.
- **Probability with real data:** conditional probability of COVID-19 infection in Manhattan from NYC case counts.
- **Combinatorics and recursion:** counting the ways to make change with a recursive algorithm.
- **Tools:** Python, Jupyter, NumPy, matplotlib.
